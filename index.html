Privacy Policy for FBA Master Quick View

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the FBA Master Quick View browser extension developed by fbamaster.dev ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to maintaining the trust of our users and safeguarding your privacy.

1. Overview of Functionality

FBA Master Quick View is a productivity tool designed to help marketplace sellers view variation breakdowns, inspect stock levels, compare seller distribution, and export on-page listing details into CSV spreadsheets. All core processing happens entirely inside your local browser session.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, residential address, email address, payment details, or Amazon account credentials.
  • On-Page Catalog Data: The extension reads publicly accessible marketplace product elements (such as ASINs, variation attributes, pricing, offer counts, and visible seller stock levels) exclusively to display them within the extension UI and generate local CSV exports. This data never leaves your machine.
  • Local Storage: We utilize Chrome's local storage APIs (chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.sync) exclusively to store your personal user preferences, table view settings, and export options.
  • No Web Tracking: The extension does not record, track, or monitor your web browsing activity or history across any non-supported pages.

3. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances. User data is never used for advertising, credit scoring, or automated profiling.
